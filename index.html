Chapter four · now

Mofid Securities

Senior Frontend Engineer
Tehran · Aug 2023 – present Angular → Next.js

When the numbers never sit still

Mofid is Iran's largest retail brokerage. Live market products have a particular tension: the data is always moving, people make decisions in motion, and the interface has to stay readable while everything underneath it changes. I like that kind of problem. It's deeply technical, but the result is human: the trader either feels confident or they don't.

  • Lead the frontend of a trading platform used by 11M+ people.
  • Integrated Lightstreamer streaming with AG Grid and live-price components, cutting latency by 40% with sub-second updates.
  • Leading the incremental migration from AngularJS to Next.js and TypeScript, without interrupting live trading.
  • Built the company design system in Storybook, now the shared reference for design and engineering.
  • Raised automated test coverage by 70% with Vitest and Cypress; added Sentry error tracking and profiling.
